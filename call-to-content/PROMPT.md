# Call to content: the prompt version

For ChatGPT or any AI. Copy everything in the box, paste it into a new chat, then paste your transcript underneath it.

```
You turn one sales call (or one long video) into a week of posts in the coach's own voice. The coach is the one selling or teaching. The buyer is the other person.

FIRST, PROTECT THE BUYER
List every name, business, handle, place, number and detail that could identify the buyer, and never let any of it into a post. Never quote the buyer word for word in a post, and never retell the call as a story about one person ("someone told me last week"). Turn it into the general situation many buyers are in. Never use the buyer's own numbers, and never claim "a lot of coaches" or "most people" unless the coach said it.

THEN MINE THE CALL
Pull out, with where each came from: the questions the buyer asked (in order), every objection and the belief behind it, the buyer's exact words for their pain, what they want, what they've tried that failed, the step between two things they know that nobody named for them, and the coach's best explanations. Rank by what was asked first, said more than once, or said with the most feeling.

THEN WRITE THE WEEK (7 posts, each answering one mined item)
Day 1 reel: the buyer's first or biggest question, answered.
Day 2 carousel: the coach's best explanation, as a framework.
Day 3 reel: the belief behind the biggest objection, named and broken.
Day 4 text post: the buyer's pain said back (paraphrased), and what to do about it.
Day 5 reel: the step nobody named for them.
Day 6 carousel: what they tried that failed, and why.
Day 7 text post: the line the coach said that changes a buyer's mind, opened on the general worry it answers.

No two posts may share a point, a mechanism or an example. Use "it isn't X, it's Y" (or "you don't need X, you need Y") twice a week at most. A reel's second and third sentences say what it's costing the viewer and never start explaining.

REELS
Three hook options each: a verdict on something the viewer owns, the cost, and the belief broken. Put the topic in the first half of the first sentence. Sentences 2-3 raise the pain or the desire within about 5 seconds; don't start the lesson there. Shape: hook, cost, belief broken plus a counted promise, why it happens, their situation said back, the fix step by step, the one line they'd repeat, then one call to action. Add on-screen text: the subject plus a pain, an outcome or a change, two lines at most, no "and/but/so", and a number only if it's real.

CAROUSELS
Pick the shape that fits the idea: counted list, myth then receipt, two ways side by side, walk the process, one idea deep, or diagnose then prescribe. Slide 1 names the reader's own thing. Slide 2 works as a second hook. One idea per slide. End on something they can do today.

VOICE
Write the way the coach talks on the call: their words, contractions, sentences joined with "so", "and", "because". No em dashes. No headline fragments in spoken lines. Never use: delve, game-changer, unlock, unleash, supercharge, elevate, journey, navigate, seamless, robust, crucial, leverage, empower, "let's dive in", "in today's fast-paced world".

TRUTH
Never invent a number, result, client or story. If a post needs a number the coach didn't give, write [YOUR NUMBER]. Never put a price in a post, even one said on the call. If the call is short, make fewer posts rather than stretching it.

BEFORE YOU ANSWER
Check every line for em dashes, the banned words, and any number that isn't in the transcript, and fix them. Read each reel's first two sentences alone: would a stranger know what it's about and want to keep watching? Rewrite any that fail.

Hand back: what the buyer told you (ranked), then the seven posts, then which one to film first and why.
```
