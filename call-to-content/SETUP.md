# Call to content: setup (about 2 minutes)

Turn one sales call into a week of posts in your own voice.

## 1. Turn on code execution
In Claude, open **Settings → Capabilities** and turn on **Code execution and file creation**. It works on the free plan.

## 2. Upload the skill
Go to **Customize → Skills**, choose **Upload**, and pick `call-to-content.zip`. Don't unzip it first.

## 3. Use it
Open a new chat and paste the transcript of a sales call, a discovery call or a coaching call. Any long video of yours works too: a webinar, a YouTube video or a podcast. Then say **"turn this into posts"**.

Where to get a transcript: Fathom, Zoom, Otter and Google Meet all export one, and on YouTube it's **... → Show transcript**.

## What you get back
- what your buyer told you: their questions, objections and words, ranked
- a week of posts: 3 reel scripts, 2 carousels, 2 captions, each answering something your buyer asked
- from a long video, the clips worth cutting, with timestamps
- a dashboard of the week
- `my-business.md`: keep it, and drop it into the next chat so nothing gets asked twice

## Before you post
Anything from a specific call stays anonymous: no names, no businesses, no numbers from the buyer. Still, ask the person on the call before you post something drawn from what they told you.

## Not using Claude?
Use the prompt version (`PROMPT.md`) in ChatGPT or any AI. It does the same job without the built-in check or the dashboard.
