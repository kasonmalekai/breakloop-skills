# The ad engine: the prompt version

For ChatGPT or any AI. Copy everything in the box into a new chat, fill in the lines at the top, and paste your best posts underneath if you have them.

```
I sell: <what you sell, and who buys it>
My ads send people to: <the free training, webinar, resource or call>
One real result: <who, what, over how long, or "none yet">

Write me a batch of ten Meta ads: seven or eight videos and two or three static images.

FIRST, THE ANGLES
An angle is a reason to buy, for one person, at one stage of awareness. Ads that give the same reason to the same person are one ad, however different they look, and Meta treats them as one. Find ten different angles from what I told you: each result I deliver, each reason my buyers give for not buying, the usual fix that fails them, the moment they realise they need help, and what I do differently. Use at least three different people and spread them across four formats: the question answered, the objection taken apart, direct response, and a real testimonial (only if I gave one). Rank them, and mark the three to five to launch first.

IF I PASTED POSTS
Find the one that beat my usual views by about twice or more. Keep its first five seconds word for word, and build most of the videos behind that opening, each with a new body that pays off the opening. Test two new openings on the strongest body. If nothing beat my usual clearly, say so and use the angles instead.

EACH VIDEO (20 to 40 seconds, me on camera)
Three hook options, each a different kind (a verdict, the cost, a broken belief), with the topic in the first half of the first sentence. On-screen text for the first frame. The script: the hook, what it's costing them by five seconds, one reason said plainly, my real result if I have one, then one call to action. A filming note: the camera, the place, the format. No two videos in the same place with the same camera.

EACH AD'S COPY
Primary text in three short lines: the hook as a statement, the proof or the reason it works, the call to action. Never lead with a question or my name. A headline of 40 characters at most.

EACH STATIC
The words on the image (a headline of 12 words at most), one idea, readable in two seconds on a phone, plus its primary text and headline.

META'S RULES AND MINE
"You" can name my buyer's work, never their money, health, weight or identity. Never promise the viewer a result; my real result goes in labelled with who, how long, and "results vary". No price anywhere. No number I didn't give you (write [YOUR NUMBER]). "Live", "limited" or a date only if I confirm it (write [CONFIRM: ...]). No em dashes, and no words like delve, game-changer, unlock, elevate, seamless, crucial, leverage.

Before you answer, check every ad against these rules and fix what breaks them.
```
