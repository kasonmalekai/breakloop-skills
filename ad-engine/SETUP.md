# The ad engine: setup (about 2 minutes)

Ten new Meta ads from your offer or your best post: video scripts in your voice, the primary text and headlines written, and the static images designed and ready to upload.

## 1. Turn on file creation
In Claude, open **Settings → Capabilities** and make sure **Create and edit files** is on. Skills need it.

## 2. Upload the skill
Go to **claude.ai/plugins**, click **Add**, then **Upload plugin**, pick `ad-engine.zip` and click **Upload**. Don't unzip it first.

## 3. Use it
Open a new chat and say **"build me a batch of ads"**, then give it one of these:
- **your offer:** what you sell, who buys it, where your ads send people, and one real result a client got;
- **or your best posts:** paste the words of your best few posts or ads with their views (or cost per lead, for ads). It finds the opening that has proved itself and builds the batch behind it.

It will ask you a few things the ads depend on, so have these handy (skip any you don't know):
- what people get from your free training or your call;
- one real client result, worded the way you share it;
- what buyers ask most before they buy, and what almost stops them;
- what you do that others don't, and who it isn't for;
- your best ad so far and its cost per lead, if you've run ads;
- where you can film;
- your name, handle and brand colour (a hex code) for the images.

It saves your answers in `my-business.md`, so next time it asks nothing twice.

## What you get back
- the plan: ten angles, each a reason to buy for one person, ranked
- ten ads: seven or eight video scripts with three hooks each, on-screen text and a filming note, plus two or three statics
- the primary text and headline for every ad
- the statics as finished images (1080 by 1350), zipped
- which three to five to launch first, and how to read the first week
- a dashboard of all of it, and `my-business.md` to keep for next time

## Not using Claude?
Use the prompt version (`PROMPT.md`) in ChatGPT or any AI. You get the scripts and copy; the statics need Claude.
