# Funnel x-ray: the prompt version

For ChatGPT or any AI. Copy everything in the box into a new chat, fill in the two lines at the top, then add the competitor's page text and screenshots of their ads underneath.

```
I sell: <what you sell, and who buys it>
My call to action: <the one thing you ask people to do>

You read a competitor's funnel like an operator, then rebuild the model for me. Take the model, never the copy.

READ THEIR FUNNEL, STAGE BY STAGE
The ad (what it sells, to whom, where it sends people), the registration page (headline, date, video, form, true claims, pain, what you'll learn), the thank-you page (the main video, the steps, the short videos answering doubts), the show-up emails and texts, the event, the offer and close, and what happens after (replay, no-show follow-up, ads for people who didn't show). For each stage say what they do, with the evidence, why it works, and where it leaks. Mark any stage you can't see as unseen.

READ THEIR ADS
Longest-running first: how long an ad has run is the best public proof it pays. For each ad: the start date, the format, the hook quoted, the angle (the reason to buy, for which person), where it sends people, and whether it chases people who already signed up or didn't show. About three live ads is a weak signal; around thirty means they're scaling.

SORT IT
What's working (specific, most important first). Take the model: the stages, the order, the angles, the formats. Never take: their words, claims, proof, numbers, offer, price and design. The one thing doing the most work, in one line. Say what works only because they're famous.

BUILD MY VERSION
Three ads, one per long-running angle, rebuilt for my buyer: three hook options each (the topic in the first half of the first sentence), on-screen text, and a 20-40 second script. The first screen of my registration page. My thank-you page: the main video outline, two or three steps, and three short videos, one per doubt my buyer has. A no-show ad that never shames.

OFF LIMITS
Any phrase, claim, number or result of theirs. Their name, offer, price or look. A price anywhere. "Live", "limited" or a date unless I confirm it (write [CONFIRM: ...]). Any number or result I didn't give you (write [YOUR NUMBER]). Em dashes, and words like delve, game-changer, unlock, elevate, seamless, crucial, leverage.

Before you answer, check my version for copied phrases, borrowed numbers and the banned words, and fix them.
```
