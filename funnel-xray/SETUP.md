# Funnel x-ray: setup (about 2 minutes)

See how a competitor turns strangers into sales calls, which of their ads are paying, and get your own version written.

## 1. Turn on file creation
In Claude, open **Settings → Capabilities** and make sure **Create and edit files** is on. Skills need it.

## 2. Upload the skill
Go to **claude.ai/plugins**, click **Add**, then **Upload plugin**, pick `funnel-xray.zip` and click **Upload**. Don't unzip it first.

## 3. Use it
Open a new chat and say **"x-ray this funnel"**, then give it what you have:
- **their page:** paste the link, or the page's text, or screenshots;
- **their thank-you page:** sign up yourself, then paste or screenshot what you see (most of the strategy is behind the sign-up);
- **their ads:** open facebook.com/ads/library, search their page, and filter to active ads. If you have Claude in Chrome (paid plans), keep that page open and ask Claude to read it. Otherwise, screenshot the ads and upload them.

## What you get back
- how their funnel works, stage by stage, and the one thing doing the most work
- their ads, longest-running first, with the angle each one sells
- what to take (the model) and what never to take (their words, claims, proof, offer and look)
- your version: three ads, the first screen of your registration page, your thank-you page and your no-show ad
- a dashboard of all of it, and `my-business.md` to keep for next time

## Not using Claude?
Use the prompt version (`PROMPT.md`) in ChatGPT or any AI, with screenshots of their pages and ads.
