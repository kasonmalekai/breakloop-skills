# Viral remix: the prompt version

For ChatGPT or any AI. Copy everything in the box into a new chat, fill in the two lines at the top, then paste the reel's words underneath.

```
I sell: <what you sell, and who buys it>
My call to action: <the one thing you ask people to do>

You rebuild a reel that blew up into a version that's entirely mine, using the same shape.

FIRST, DECODE WHY IT WORKED
The hook (what it said it was about and how early, what pulled the viewer one way, what pulled them back), the on-screen text, the format, the structure beat by beat with rough seconds, the one feeling it runs on and where it peaks, the payoff, and the call to action. Then sort it into three lists: what made it work (specific, most important first), what to keep (the shape, the turns, the feeling, the timing), and what to swap (its topic, examples, proof, numbers, words and advice).

THEN REBUILD IT AS MINE
Pick my point first, from my offer: the one thing my buyer needs to hear that fits this shape. Map it beat for beat, so the same turn lands at the same moment, within about 10 seconds of the original's length. Rebuild the hook from scratch with the same kind of hook: the topic in the first half of the first sentence, a lean, a snapback. Give three hook options. Sentences two and three say what it's costing the viewer and never start explaining. Keep the same feeling. End with my call to action. Add the on-screen text (two lines at most, no "and/but/so") and a shot list one person can film alone with a phone.

Then give two more versions with the same shape, aimed at two other reasons my buyer would care: a different point each, not just different wording.

OFF LIMITS
Any phrase from the original, its advice, its numbers or its results. The creator's name, or any hint it's a remix. Any result or number I didn't give you (write [YOUR NUMBER] instead). Em dashes. Words like delve, game-changer, unlock, elevate, journey, seamless, crucial, leverage, "let's dive in". The "it isn't X, it's Y" line more than once.

BEFORE YOU ANSWER
Check every version for copied phrases, borrowed numbers and the banned words, and fix them. Read each version's first two sentences alone: would a stranger in my market stop and stay? Rewrite any that fail.
```
