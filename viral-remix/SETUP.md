# Viral remix: setup (about 2 minutes)

Turn any reel that blew up, from any niche, into your own version, ready to film.

## 1. Turn on file creation
In Claude, open **Settings → Capabilities** and make sure **Create and edit files** is on. Skills need it.

## 2. Upload the skill
Go to **claude.ai/plugins**, click **Add**, then **Upload plugin**, pick `viral-remix.zip` and click **Upload**. Don't unzip it first.

## 3. Use it
Open a new chat, paste the words of a reel that blew up (or upload 2-4 screenshots of it), and say **"remix this for me"**. Claude can't watch a video from a link, so it needs the words.

The best ones to remix come from other niches, with 3 or more times the creator's followers in views. Add the views and the follower count if you know them.

## What you get back
- why it worked: the hook, the format, the structure beat by beat, and the feeling it runs on
- your version, ready to film: three hooks, the on-screen text, the script and a shot list
- two more versions aimed at other reasons to care
- a dashboard of all of it, and `my-business.md` to keep for next time

It never copies the original's words, numbers or advice. It keeps the shape and builds the point from your own offer.

## Not using Claude?
Use the prompt version (`PROMPT.md`) in ChatGPT or any AI.
