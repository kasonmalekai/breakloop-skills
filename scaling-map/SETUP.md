# The scaling map: setup (about 2 minutes)

Paste your numbers and get the math to your next revenue number, the one stage holding your funnel back, the levers in order, and the next six weeks.

## 1. Turn on code execution
In Claude, open **Settings → Capabilities** and turn on **Code execution and file creation**. It works on the free plan.

## 2. Upload the skill
Go to **Customize → Skills**, choose **Upload**, and pick `scaling-map.zip`. Don't unzip it first.

## 3. Use it
Open a new chat and say **"build my scaling map"**. Have your last 4 weeks of numbers ready:
- new leads (opt-ins, registrants or new conversations);
- calls booked, and calls that actually happened (count every booking whose date has passed);
- sales, and the cash that actually came in per sale;
- for a webinar: how many attended live, and how many were still there at the pitch;
- if you run ads: clicks and ad spend;
- who takes your calls, and how many each can take a week;
- what you've tried in the last 90 days.

Your numbers stay in the chat; nothing is sent anywhere.

## What you get back
- the math to your next number, a month, a week and a day
- your funnel, stage by stage, against targets for your kind of traffic
- the one constraint, and what fixing it is worth a month
- the levers in order, the next six weeks one change at a time, and the mistake to avoid
- a dashboard of all of it

Run it again in 30 days with the new numbers.

## Not using Claude?
Use the prompt version (`PROMPT.md`) in ChatGPT or any AI. Check its math by hand: the Claude version does the math with a script.
