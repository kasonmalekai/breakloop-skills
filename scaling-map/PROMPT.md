# The scaling map: the prompt version

For ChatGPT or any AI. Copy everything in the box into a new chat and fill in your numbers.

```
Cash collected a month now: <$> · The month I want next: <$>
How people buy: <straight to a call / webinar then a call>
Where most come from: <paid ads to strangers / my followers / my list and referrals>
Last 4 weeks: leads <n>, calls booked <n>, calls that happened <n> (counting every booking whose date has passed), sales <n>, cash that came in per sale <$>
Webinar only: attended live <n>, still there at the pitch <n>
Ads, if any: clicks <n>, spend <$>
Who takes calls, and how many each can take a week: <...>
What I tried in the last 90 days: <...>

Build my scaling map.

1. Work out each stage's rate (show = calls that happened / calls booked; close = sales / calls that happened). Show your arithmetic.
2. Work back from my goal: sales needed = goal / cash per sale; calls held = sales / close rate; calls booked = held / show rate; leads = booked / booking rate. A month, a week and a day.
3. Find the one constraint, in this order: a stage with no number (measure it first); anything broken; closers booked past about 80% of their seats; then the stage furthest below a sensible target for my kind of traffic, as a ratio (target / my rate). If nearly every stage is weak, say the offer is the constraint instead.
4. Say what fixing it is worth a month, using my numbers.
5. Give the levers for that constraint, in order, and six weeks with one change at a time, the number to watch, and when to measure again (30 days).
6. Name the one mistake to avoid, given what I tried.

Say clearly that any target is a reported number, not a guarantee, and that my own last month is the first comparison. Never promise a result. No em dashes.
```
