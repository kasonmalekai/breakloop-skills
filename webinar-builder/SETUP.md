# The webinar builder: setup (about 2 minutes)

Your whole webinar as a PowerPoint deck, slide by slide, with what to say on each one, built to end on a booked call.

## 1. Turn on code execution
In Claude, open **Settings → Capabilities** and turn on **Code execution and file creation**. It works on the free plan.

## 2. Upload the skill
Go to **Customize → Skills**, choose **Upload**, and pick `webinar-builder.zip`. Don't unzip it first.

## 3. Use it
Open a new chat and say **"build my webinar"**, with a paragraph about your offer. It will ask what else it needs in one message, so have these handy (skip any you don't know):
- what happens on your sales call, and what people leave with even if they don't buy;
- the one thing the webinar should teach them to do;
- your method, the steps you take clients through;
- your story, as far as you're happy to tell it on camera;
- real client results, worded the way you share them, and any testimonials you can show;
- the doubts buyers raise before they buy, and who it isn't for;
- something you could give everyone who stays to the end;
- live or recorded, and whether anything is really limited;
- your name, brand colour (a hex code) and booking link.

## What you get back
- the plan: the promise, the one idea, the steps, the doubts to name early, the proof
- `deck.pptx`: about 45 to 70 slides, with what to say in the speaker notes; it opens in PowerPoint, Keynote or Google Slides
- the run of show, with the call asked for at about 45 minutes
- a list of what to gather before you go live
- a dashboard of all of it, and `my-business.md` to keep for next time

## Not using Claude?
Use the prompt version (`PROMPT.md`) in ChatGPT or any AI. You get the plan and the script, slide by slide; the PowerPoint file needs Claude.
