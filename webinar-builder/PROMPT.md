# The webinar builder: the prompt version

For ChatGPT or any AI. Copy everything in the box into a new chat and fill in the lines at the top.

```
I sell: <what you sell, and who buys it>
The call they'll book: <what happens on it, how long, what they leave with>
The webinar teaches them to: <one thing>
My method, in steps: <your steps>
Proof: <real results: who, what changed, over how long, or "none yet">
Doubts buyers raise: <two or three>
Gift for staying to the end: <something real, or "suggest one">

Write my webinar, slide by slide, built to book calls. No price, package or payment terms anywhere: the call is free and that's what's offered.

THE CLOCK
Introduction about 10 minutes, teaching 35 to 40, a short transition, then the close, with the call first asked for at 45 to 50 minutes. Then Q&A, where every answer bends back to the call.

THE INTRODUCTION
Why me (a real result), a commitment they type in the chat by minute 5, the doubts named early (each ignored, disagreed with, or agreed with and narrowed, with what to watch for), the life on the other side, what's coming today with the how held back, and the gift for staying to the end.

THE TEACHING
One path in 3 to 5 steps on one map. Show the map before step 1 and again after every step. Step 1 gets about a third of the time. Every step: why it matters, what their week looks like once it's done, the how, and one small commitment. For each step, name the part that's slow or hard alone, and let the close offer help with it. Only true gaps; never hold back what a slide promised.

THE TRANSITION AND CLOSE
A fast recap, one honest question (would you like help going further?), then: the call, who's on it, what happens, what they leave with; that it's free and how long; ask. What comes with booking, one at a time, "so you can...", the gift last. Take the risk out of the call. Money and time doubts first, two answers each, asking again after each. Show the booking page.

EVERY SLIDE
One idea, readable in 3 seconds, with speaker notes: what to say, what the slide is for, what to ask the chat. Real results only, with their qualifier beside them. "Live" or "limited" only if true. Where a screenshot or testimonial should go, write [ASSET NEEDED: what]. Anything I didn't tell you, write [CONFIRM: what]. No em dashes, and no words like delve, game-changer, unlock, elevate, seamless, crucial, leverage.

Before you answer, count the slides, add up the minutes, and check the call is asked for at 45 to 50.
```
